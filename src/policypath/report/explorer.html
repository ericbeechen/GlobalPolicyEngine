Session time machine

Any session, as the engine saw it at that close: the path the market priced and the path the rule gave with that day's inputs, the gap at the traded meeting against the band its z is measured on, and the side each sleeve's rule decided.

Who closes the gap

When the market and the rule disagree at the traded meeting, which one moves? Each session's gap, less its trailing mean, is binned into deciles, and each point is how far the market's rate, the rule's rate and the policy rate moved for that same meeting over the next h sessions. A rule line sloping up, or a market line sloping down, is that side closing the gap. From closure.md.
