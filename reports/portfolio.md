## How the book is built

**Calendar.** The book runs on USD's sessions. A sleeve's P&L on its own sessions is credited to the next book session on or after it, so a sterling session on a US holiday is summed into the next US session and a US session on a UK holiday gets nothing from the sterling sleeves (the stale sessions are counted below). Positions are decided only at book closes; a sleeve reaches a decision at its next own session and executes it a session later, as week 8's sleeves do.

**Covariance.** Of the sleeves' unit P&L (+1 book DV01 on the first leg), strictly before each close: EWMA weights lambda^age, lambda = 0.97 (effective sample 66 sessions), zero mean, complete rows only. The non-synchronous term: London closes before New York, so a USD move after the London close reaches the sterling sleeves a day later. S = S0 + (S1 + S1')/2, S1 the EWMA of x_t x_(t-1)': the Newey-West estimator at one lag. The unweighted S0 + S1 + S1' is not positive definite on 905 of the 3,676 sessions from the book's first decision (a variance below zero on 5); the Bartlett-weighted S is on 3,676 of them. The largest contributor is GBP - USD 2y: without it the unweighted form fails on 209 sessions. Every variance below zero is GBP outright's, marked at one close, so its negative lag-1 autocorrelation is its own P&L's, not the closes' timing. The lag term's diagonal is each sleeve's own lag-1 autocovariance, so S's diagonal is a one-lag long-run variance for every sleeve, not a timing correction only. Shrunk toward its diagonal: Sigma = (1 - delta) S + delta diag(S), delta = sum over i != j of pi_ij / n_eff, over sum over i != j of S_ij^2, clipped to [0, 1], pi_ij = sum w (x_i x_j - S0_ij)^2, n_eff = 1 / sum w^2: a Ledoit-Wolf-type intensity with Schafer and Strimmer's target D (the diagonal, unequal variances). The sd each sleeve is sized on is the diagonal's, floored at 0.5 x its trailing two-year median as week 8's, and the covariance is rescaled to the floored sds with its correlations kept.

**Constructions**, on the sleeves with a side (g, the hysteresis side under the ELB treatment): inverse-vol (the headline, proposed) w = g / sd; ERC, the signs fixed by g and every sleeve's share of the ex-ante variance equal (cyclical coordinate descent to 1e-10; one sleeve is inverse-vol); mean-variance w = Sigma^-1 mu, mu = g x min(|z|, 3) x sd (the z as expected return, so with no correlation it is inverse-vol weighted by |z|). Mean-variance on the diagonal of the same Sigma (no correlations) is run beside it, to split its difference from inverse-vol into its |z| weights and its correlations.

**Size.** Scaled to 5% of capital a year ex ante, sqrt(w' Sigma w x 252); the gross DV01, every leg's |q|, capped at 0.4% of capital per bp (USD 400k per bp); a sleeve re-sized only on a new side or when its target moves more than 10% from the position held, unless keeping it would break the cap. Costs on every leg's change, week 8's. The book decides from the first close with 250 complete sessions of unit P&L behind it (2012-03-01); flat at the ELB it has nothing counted before Jan 2014.

**Drawdown control** (reported, not used): half size (0.5) from the close after the drawdown (peak less equity, over capital) exceeds 10%, full again when it is back under 5%; decided at the close and executed a session later, like everything else.

Sharpe is mean over sd of daily net P&L on the counted sessions, annualised by the book calendar's observed sessions a year, with its SE, sqrt((1 + SR^2/2)/years), in brackets: the least the uncertainty can be. Two books over the same sessions share most of their positions, so a difference between them is judged against the SE of the difference, paired on the sessions both count (Jobson and Korkie's, with Memmel's correction: sqrt((2 (1 - rho) + (s_a^2 + s_b^2 - 2 s_a s_b rho^2) / 2) / n), s the per-session Sharpes, rho the correlation of the two daily net P&Ls, annualised), not against either Sharpe's own SE.

## The covariance as a forecast

Paleologo's evaluation of a risk model (*The Elements of Quantitative Investing*, 2024, ch. 5), `strategy/scoring.py`: it changes nothing the book does. The configured covariance (lambda 0.97, the lag term, shrunk, floored: the one the book sizes on) is scored beside lambda 0.94, lambda 0.99, unshrunk, no lag term and no floor, each one choice moved off it. Every forecast is scored at the book's horizon: the matrix a close sizes on against the unit P&L of the session its position is held into, 2 sessions later, over the 3,520 sessions from Mar 2012 on which every sleeve is credited exactly one session of its own (no stale or catch-up sessions). The test portfolios: each sleeve alone; 200 random portfolios a session in the book's risk units (Procedure 5.1, u / sd, u ~ N(0, I), a fixed seed); the headline's targets as decided (before the band and the execution); and realized alpha (Procedure 5.3): over 167 non-overlapping 21-session blocks, Sigma^-1 times the block's mean unit P&L, against the block's sample covariance. That tests the precision matrix mean-variance reads; for Gaussian P&L a block's sample mean and covariance are independent, so a correct Sigma scores 1, and it reads the future by design.

Realized over ex-ante is sqrt(mean(r / h)), r the portfolio's squared P&L and h its forecast: what a book holding it at the forecast's risk would see. QLIKE, r/h - log(r/h) - 1, and MSE, (r - h)^2, are the losses Patton (2011) shows rank forecasts correctly through a noisy realized variance; each candidate's mean less the configured one's (negative is better; MSE as a ratio) carries its Diebold-Mariano t, Newey-West with Bartlett weights to 21 sessions (none for the blocks, which do not overlap). Each sleeve alone cannot tell shrinkage apart: it moves only the correlations.

### What realized over ex-ante is made of

A book sized on an estimate runs above its ex-ante vol even where the estimate is right on average: it puts on more where the estimate is low, and fat tails make the estimate noisier. So the same estimate and the same portfolios are run on P&L with no vol dynamics, 20 draws of each: iid Gaussian (the noise alone), and iid rows of the sleeves' own shocks, whitened by the configured 1-step covariance and rescaled to an identity (their tails and co-movement, not their clustering). Realized over ex-ante then factors as noise x tails x the vol dynamics the EWMA does not track and, for the book, x the band, the cap, the calendar and the costs: the book as run is its realized over its ex-ante vol on the sessions with a position, as in the answer. Kurtosis is of the P&L standardised by its forecast (3 if Gaussian). The benchmark is ex post: it pools the whole sample's shocks.

## Checks (the build stops if one fails)

- ERC's risk contributions are equal on every session it sizes two or more sleeves, to 1e-8; on the 453 sessions with one active sleeve ERC is inverse-vol.
- The covariance each construction reads is positive definite on every session it sizes.
- The gross DV01 cap holds on every close; DV01 traded by cause adds up; the book's P&L is its sleeves'.
- Nothing is earned or charged on a session the ELB treatment leaves out.
- Every book on the headline's sleeves counts the headline's sessions, so the paired differences between them add up (mean-variance's two channels sum to its difference from inverse-vol).
- The drawdown control's scale is the fixed point of its state machine on the book's own equity (2 rounds for the headline).
- The configured covariance the forecast section scores is the matrix the book sizes on, bit for bit.
