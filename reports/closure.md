# Who closes the gap: the market or the rule

Generated by `scripts/build_closure.py` from `data/panel/`. Do not edit by hand. Methods: `src/policypath/closure.py`; decisions: notes/DECISIONS.md, "Who closes the gap".

- Panels: USD 2011-03-04 .. 2026-10-15; GBP 2010-08-26 .. 2026-10-15
- The meeting: the fourth ahead at each close (the one the outrights trade), held as the path moves on; a session whose meeting has left the path by the end of its window is left out.
- The regressor: the gap at that meeting less its trailing window's mean at the close (the edge the z trades), on sessions with a z outside the ELB state.
- The changes, bp: the market's rate for that meeting, the rule's rate for it, and the policy rate in force, from the session after the close to h sessions after that, as the IC measures them.

The gap is market minus rule, so a negative slope on the market, or a positive one on the rule, is that side closing the gap. The gap's own slope is the market's less the rule's; the share closed is minus it, and the rule's share is its part of that. Each slope has its Newey-West t (Bartlett weights to lag h) in brackets; the non-overlapping range fits every h-th session from each of the h start offsets.
