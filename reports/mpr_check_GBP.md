# The GBP path against the Bank of England's MPR conditioning paths

Generated by `scripts/check_mpr.py`. Do not edit by hand. Ground truth: `tests/data/boe/conditioning_paths.csv` (the Bank's Projections Databank, July 2026).

- Reports compared: 29, 87 quarters inside the path's eight meetings
- All reports: mean difference -0.46bp, mean absolute 1.00bp, worst 7.22bp
- Reports whose window was read in the report itself (7): worst quarter 4.64bp

Where they differ: the Bank averages its smooth instantaneous forward curve, which starts a priced move before the meeting it belongs to; the path is a step function that moves on the meeting's effective date. So inside a quarter where a hike is priced the Bank's average comes out higher, and a cut lower: the difference correlates -0.59 with the move priced inside the quarter (-0.030bp per bp). Quarters with less than 5bp priced inside them agree to 0.83bp on average (26 quarters), the rest to 1.07bp. The rest is data: April and July 2026 are stored by the Bank to one decimal (up to 5bp of rounding), November 2022 used a 7-day window, and windows not read in the report itself were scraped.
