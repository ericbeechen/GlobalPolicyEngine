# EUR data-availability spike (ECB Data Portal, pulled 2026-09-28)

> Timeboxed data spike, week 6 day 1. Numbers are from the ECB Data Portal pulls of 2026-09-28; the MP-dated frequency was re-checked by hand the same day. Raw pulls were not committed: every one is reproducible from the commands at the end.

## Verdict
**MMSR MP-dated OIS cannot support a daily 8-meeting path.** The ECB publishes it only as **quarterly** aggregates (FREQ=Q) of CCP-cleared trades. Each quarter holds one volume-weighted rate per MP bucket: MP1..MP8 of the current year (SETTL_YEAR 0) or of the next year (SETTL_YEAR 1). A quarter has 4-9 non-empty buckets (2020-22: 4-8). There are no daily MP-dated quotes at all. MMSR's "B" OIS series are also not daily. They hold one observation per maintenance period, dated at the MP end: 76 obs from 2017-03-14 to 2026-07-28. The only daily public OIS data is EMMS: four wide spot buckets, banks only, released once a year.
**What it can support:** a low-frequency cross-check. That means a per-MP average of spot OIS 1M-12M, plus quarterly averages of forward MP-dated rates. **For a daily path, use Euribor futures (ICE, on Databento IFLL.IMPACT since 2018-12) minus a Euribor-€STR basis.** Anchor it on €STR and the DFR. CME €STR futures only start 2022-10-31, so they cannot cover 2020-22.

**HICP change:** the new `HICP` dataflow (DATA_PROVIDER `4D0`) replaces `ICP`, whose last observation is 2025-12. Use `HICP` from 2026 on. The XEF series is labelled "excluding energy, food, alcohol and tobacco".

**Publication lags (bounded with `updatedAfter`):**
- MMSR B, MP ending 2026-07-28: published between 08-18 and 08-21, about 3 weeks.
- MMSR Q, 2026-Q2: published after 08-01, about 5+ weeks.
- EMMS: whole history re-released once, between 2026-03-01 and 05-01. Data end at 2025-12, so the lag is up to about 15 months.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 6 | 7 | 6 | 6 |

- Volumes are EUR mn per quarter. In 2020-21 most buckets hold EUR 5-270bn. 2022 rises to about EUR 1.2tn.
- Forward coverage usually reaches only about 4-6 MPs ahead. The 1-year-ahead buckets are often empty.

## Futures (web, not pulled)
- **CME €STR 3M futures (ESR):** first trade date 2022-10-31. They trade on CME Globex, so they should be in Databento `GLBX.MDP3` from that date. Liquidity is thin; most €STR futures trading happens on Eurex (not checked).
- **ICE Euribor futures:** Databento dataset **`IFLL.IMPACT`** ("ICE Europe Financials", launched 2025-12-02, history from 2018-12-23). The dataset `IFEU.IMPACT` is ICE Europe *Commodities* and is the wrong one.

## Reproduce
```
B=https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service
curl "$B/dataflow/ECB"                                                 # list dataflows
curl "$B/dataflow/ECB/MMSR/latest?references=all&detail=full"          # DSD and codelists
curl "$B/data/MMSR/......O........?detail=serieskeysonly&format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/MMSR/B.U2._X._Z.S1ZV._Z.O._X.AT+TT+WR._X..._Z.EUR._Z?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/MMSR/Q.U2._X._Z.S125E1._Z.O._X.AT+TT+WR._X.MP+IM...EUR._Z?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/EMMS/.......O..WR+MFD+IS12+OIS12.........?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/EST/B.EU000A2X2A25.WT?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/FM/D.U2.EUR.4F.KR.DFR.LEV?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/YC/B.U2.EUR.4F.G_N_A.SV_C_YM.SR_3M?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/HICP/M.U2.N.XEF000.4D0.ANR?format=csvdata"
curl "$B/data/MMSR/<key>?format=csvdata&updatedAfter=2026-08-18T00:00:00"   # bound the publication lag
```
- Keys with the wrong number of dimensions return an HTML "access blocked" page with status 400, not an SDMX error.
