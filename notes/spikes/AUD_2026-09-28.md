# AUD data-scoping spike (2026-09-28)

> Half-day scoping spike, run after EUR failed the bar. The RBA tables were pulled; the ASX, Databento and ABS facts are from the linked pages, not from data. The files named in parentheses were working copies and were not committed.

## 1. ASX 30 Day Interbank Cash Rate Futures (IB)
Source: ASX factsheet https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/markets/trade-our-derivatives-market/derivatives-market-overview/interest-rate-derivatives/short-term-derivatives/30-day-interbank-cash-rate-factsheet.pdf (ib_factsheet.txt)
and settlement example https://www.asx.com.au/content/dam/asx/markets/trade-our-derivatives-market/derivatives-market-overview/short-term-derivatives/30-Day-Interbank-Cash-Rate-Futures-factsheet.pdf (ib_settle_example.txt)
- Settlement: 100 - (sum of daily RBA Interbank Overnight Cash Rate (OCR, a.k.a. AONIA) over the contract month / number of CALENDAR days); weekends/holidays carry previous business day's rate. Rounded to 0.001 (since Mar-2008 expiry). Last trading day = last business day of the month, 4:30pm. => identical structure to ZQ (calendar-month average of overnight rate). Backend reusable as-is.
- Strip: monthly contracts up to 18 months ahead; official market makers quote all 18 months. 18 months ≈ 12 meetings (8/yr) or ≈16 (11/yr) -> covers 8 meetings.
- Tick 0.005 (half bp); 1bp = A$24.66 on A$3m notional. Liquidity concentrated in first ~6-12 months; back months are MM-quoted (daily settlement prices exist but may be quote-driven).
- Rate definition: no break in settlement rule. The underlying OCR calculation changed 9 May 2016 (survey of participants -> RITS transaction data); "AONIA" is the benchmark name for the same RBA cash rate. IMPORTANT: IB settles on OCR, not the target. OCR - target spread (from RBA F1): 0bp 2008-2019; 2020 mean -7.8bp; 2021 -6.9; 2022 -4.2; 2023 -3.0; 2024 -2.0; 2025 -0.7; 2026 0. Backend needs a time-varying OCR-target basis (observable daily from F1, so easy to handle).

  "Historical Data – Internal Use Fee": 07700221 ASX 24 Daily Summary — A$2,760 per year of history, once-off (excl GST). 07700192 ASX 24 Derivatives MDP (binary) A$11,500/yr. So 2010-2025 (16 yrs) of daily summary ≈ A$44k ex-GST; 2016+ ≈ A$28k. (Need to confirm Daily Summary contains settlement price per IB expiry — likely, it is ASX 24's EOD file.)
- ASX website: settlement-history page gives FINAL expiry settlement only, ~2021+, no downloads.
- Nasdaq Data Link (Quandl) CHRIS/ASX_IB1..: CHRIS deprecated/no longer updated; continuous-contract only (not individual expiries); API blocked by Incapsula when probed.
- Investing.com "AUD 30 day Interbank" / TradingView ASX24:IB: generic front/continuous, scrape-only, ToS issues; TradingView expired contracts need paid plan + ASX24 data add-on. Not research-grade.
- Bloomberg (IBA <Cmdty>) / Refinitiv (0#YIB:) have full history — not accessible per brief.

## 3. Free official alternative: RBA F1 OIS
Files: f1-data.csv (https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/tables/csv/f1-data.csv, 2011-01-04 -> 2026-09-28), f01dhist.xls (https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/tables/xls-hist/f01dhist.xls, to 2010-12-31)
- FIRMMOIS1D / FIRMMOIS3D / FIRMMOIS6D (1/3/6-month OIS, daily, closing): 2001-07-02 -> 2022-12-01. "Publication of this series was discontinued in December 2022." Source Tullett Prebon to Apr-2021, FENICS after.
- Also FIRMMCRTD (target), FIRMMCRID (OCR, from 1976), FIRMMCCRT (changes), BAB/NCD 1/3/6m (ASX EOD BBSW-like, credit/term premium).
- 6-month max horizon = ~4-5 meetings (11/yr); cannot give 8 meetings; and stops Dec-2022.
- F17 zero-coupon AGS curve (f17-yields.csv, f17hist.xlsx): daily 0/0.25/0.5/.../10y; only 2017-01-03 onward (earlier history "removed for commercial reasons"); government-bond not OIS, distorted by YCC 2020-21 and bond-bill basis. Not a policy-path instrument.
- F2 = gov bond yields 2y+ (not useful for meeting path).

## 4. RBA meeting regime
- 11 meetings/yr (first Tue of month except Jan) until 2023; 8/yr from Feb 2024 (two-day meetings, decision 2:30pm day 2). Sources: https://www.rba.gov.au/media-releases/2023/mr-23-18.html , https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-02-05/rba-meetings-changing-what-it-means-mortgages/103378372 . Monetary Policy Board replaced Reserve Bank Board for policy from Mar-2025 (same 8/yr cadence).
- Effective date: since Feb-2008 announced 2:30pm meeting day, effective next business day (F1 notes). Confirmed in F1: e.g. 4-Mar-2020, 4-Nov-2020 (0.10), 4-May-2022 (0.35). Out-of-cycle meeting 19-Mar-2020 -> effective 20-Mar-2020 (0.25).
- ELB: target 0.25 (20-Mar-2020) then 0.10 (4-Nov-2020 to 3-May-2022); OCR actually printed 0.03-0.04 (below target). YCC on 3y AGS (0.25% Mar-2020, 0.10% Nov-2020; abandoned Nov-2021).
- Full target change list 2011-2026 in f1-data.csv (latest: hikes Feb/Mar/May 2026 to 4.35).

## 5. Real-time macro
- ABS CPI quarterly until 2025; Monthly CPI Indicator (partial basket) from late-2022 (data back to 2018); complete Monthly CPI from 26-Nov-2025 (Oct-2025 ref month), Indicator discontinued. https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-statements/abs-announces-monthly-cpi-start-november-2025 , RBA SMP Nov-2025 Box C.
- ALFRED/FRED: Australian series are OECD MEI re-publications (e.g. LRHUTTTTAUM156S, AUSCPIALLQINMEI); ALFRED vintages start ~2013 and track OECD releases, not ABS first prints — not a usable real-time source.
- Better: Australian Real-Time Macroeconomic Database (Univ. Melbourne, https://fbe.unimelb.edu.au/economics/macrocentre/artmdatabase — page returned 403, update status unverified), or build vintages from ABS release calendar + ABS past-release spreadsheets. Implication: pre-2022 inflation nowcast info arrives quarterly (~4 weeks after quarter end), with monthly labour force as the main monthly signal; this means the AUD nowcast needs a mixed-frequency/quarterly block and its own vintage builder.

## Verdict
Instrument: yes, IB is a clean ZQ analogue (calendar-day monthly average of OCR, 18 months listed, well past 8 meetings) and drops into the futures least-squares backend. Extra work: an OCR-target basis term (-7 to -2bp from 2020 to 2024, taken from F1) and a meeting calendar that switches from 11 to 8 meetings a year in 2024. The obstacle is DATA: no Databento feed and no free per-expiry history. The realistic paths are the ASX 24 Daily Summary history at A$2,760 per year (about A$44k ex-GST for 2010-25), or Bloomberg/Refinitiv through an institution. The free fallback is RBA F1 1/3/6m OIS, 2001-07 to 2022-12: daily, but only about 4-5 meetings deep and discontinued, so it can't deliver an 8-meeting path.
