-- CRITICAL | §11.4 cross-country instrument, §5.2 portfolio covariance, §6 decompose
ISSUE: The cross sleeve trades the same two instruments as the USD and GBP outright sleeves (the k-th meeting ZQ month and OIS forward, both in book DV01 at spot). Its unit P&L is therefore GBP_out - USD_out on common sessions. The 5x5 sleeve covariance is rank-deficient (about rank 4). Mean-variance (Sigma^-1 mu) and ERC become ill-posed and only the shrinkage keeps them invertible. The spec still pre-writes the conclusion 'with five sleeves shrinkage barely matters', which this construction makes false. The level vs cross-country split in week 10 then depends on how the weights spread one exposure across collinear sleeves.
EVIDENCE: §11.4: 'DV01-matched spread of each currency's k-th meeting outright instrument (ZQ month / OIS forward)'. §3.1: outright = futures_month / curve_forward at k = backtest.horizon, the same k the differential uses. §1.4 applies the same fx = S_t/S_p to every GBP leg. Summed over GBP sessions between two common sessions, the outright P&L telescopes to the cross's GBP leg unless the instrument rolls in between. Also, with mu_s = z_s sigma_s, MV reduces exactly to inverse-vol (z/sigma) when Sigma is diagonal, so MV vs inverse-vol differs only through the near-singular off-diagonal terms.
REC: Either (a) build the portfolio over legs/instruments (USD ZQ, GBP forward, USD 2s10s, GBP 2s10s): sleeves are signal sources whose DV01s net into leg positions, Sigma is 4x4 and well-posed, and attribution goes by sleeve signal. Do not pre-write the shrinkage conclusion: report headline Sharpe with and without shrinkage plus the condition number of Sigma. State that MV = inverse-vol + correlation terms.

-- MAJOR | §1.3 attribution identity
ISSUE: The identity cannot do what the plan asks: 'if the identity holds, the signs are right'. For par and forward legs, 'total from marks' is a DV01-linear mark (-q x (held_t - held_p) x 100 plus the same accrued carry) that reads the same interpolated curve points as the components. So total = roll + rate + carry holds by telescoping. A flipped carry sign, an inverted FX quote, or a flipped signal-to-side mapping moves both sides equally and passes. The spec's line 'A flipped sign anywhere breaks the identity' is false. Only separate roll/rate sign flips are caught.
EVIDENCE: §1.3: total = [-(held_t - held_p) x 100 + carry_unfx] x fx, while carry = +(...) x fx uses the same carry. roll + rate = -(held_t - held_p) x 100 x fx identically. For ZQ, held_t is 100 - P_t, the same price the 'price-based' total uses.
REC: Keep the exact linear identity (1e-9) as bookkeeping, and add an independent full-revaluation total with a tolerance set by the stated convexity bound. A par bond struck at p with coupon y_p(T), priced by the standard bond formula at y_t(T - Δ/365), plus coupon accrual minus funding. A forward OIS struck at K = F_p valued as N x tau x DF_t x (F_t - K). A carry or FX sign error then shows up as a residual of about 2x carry, far above convexity. Add an end-to-end sign test per sleeve: market converges to the model, so the signal's side makes money. Correct the docstring and the DECISIONS claim.

OVERALL: The sign conventions are mostly right, and so is spec §11.1: the plan's week-7 example is backwards. Numerically, with overnight at 5.00%, 1y par 4.40% and 2y par 4.00%, a unit-DV01 2y payer over a quarter on an unchanged curve earns carry +(5.00-4.00) x 100 x 91/365 / 1.904 = +13.1bp plus roll +(4.10-4.00) x 100 = +10bp. The horizons also differ. Replace it with phi* = -CR_h / (edge - CR_h), the share of the gap that must close within h, compared with the historical closure rate. (3) The §1.3 identity is nearly tautological: carry, fx, the rolled rate and the ZQ DV01 all cancel, so it cannot detect a flipped direction or carry sign. -- CRITICAL | §3.1 carry.py, breakeven / 'gap covers the carry' (also the §3.1 report's 'week's insight' and positions.carry_filter)

If the gap closed to zero, as |gap| implicitly assumes, the receiver would lose. The raw gap also includes the constant offsets (r*, u*, spread) that z is built to remove. This is the bp that the signal expects to close, and each component (level, slope, differential) has its own window mean. -- MAJOR | §3.1 breakeven definition; §11.1 wording
ISSUE: The 'covers' test compares two rival scenarios over different horizons. Read as a net-of-carry test, which is how the report and the carry filter describe it, it double counts. For a fixed-window instrument (ZQ month, MPC-dated OIS forward), the identity gives total_h = carry + roll + rate with rate = -(held_t - rolled). If the market converges to the rule for the held window, held_t = M, so total = (F_p - M) + cash carry = gap. The roll cancels exactly because the gap is measured against the forward, and the forward already embeds the priced path that generates the roll. carry+roll is the P&L under a different forecast: the constant-maturity curve standing still. Scenario and horizon also do not match: the gap is a to-convergence number, while carry+roll covers 91 days. Separately, spec §11.1 should say that 'bleeding' is a mark-to-market cost in the standing-still scenario, not an expected loss if the model is right.
EVIDENCE: Derivation: roll + rate = -(held_t - held_p), so 'rolled' drops out of any convergence scenario. 'Stand still for T, then converge' also totals exactly the gap. The spec's rule |gap| > -(carry+roll) is equivalent to phi* < 1/2 in the mix below, which is an unstated 50/50 weighting.
REC: Use a horizon-consistent convex mix rather than a sum. Over h, let the held rate go a fraction phi of the way to the rule and otherwise follow the frozen curve, giving E_h = phi x edge + (1 - phi) x CR_h, where CR_h = carry + roll over h. Report phi* = -CR_h / (edge - CR_h) when CR_h < 0 (and 0 otherwise): the share of the gap that must close within a quarter to pay for the bleed. Compare phi* with the empirical phi_h, the share of the de-meaned gap that historically closed within h, estimated on an expanding window with OLS of -Delta(gap - mean) over h on (gap - mean). Call a trade 'right but bleeding' when CR_h < 0 < edge, and flag it 'does not pay for the bleed' when phi* > phi_h. This fits in two sentences and involves no double counting. For the curve sleeve, report CR_h only, or map the slope gap to a 2y yield move explicitly (see below).

-- MAJOR | §1.3 attribution identity ('A flipped sign anywhere breaks the identity'); plan week 7 'the trap'
ISSUE: As specified, the identity is nearly tautological. total = [-(held_t - held_p) x 100 + carry_unfx] x fx reuses carry_unfx and fx. 'rolled' cancels in roll + rate. ================= 27 findings
OVERALL: Weeks 8-12 of the spec build the right pieces, but three designs would give wrong or indefensible results as written, and several evaluation definitions are too loose to implement the same way twice.

(1) The cross sleeve is built from the same two instruments as the two outright sleeves. That makes the sleeve covariance matrix (near-)singular. Mean-variance and ERC become ill-posed, costs are charged on gross legs rather than net trades, and the with-vs-without-shrinkage test on the inverse-vol headline cannot fail.

(2) The ELB "flat" choice is justified by "the rule stays at the floor". The data contradict this for 742 of about 1,717 USD floor sessions (2014-15 and 2021-22) and 171 GBP sessions. The inclusive 25bp band also gates the first year after lift-off. Only 8.1 of 14.9 years stay tradeable.

(3) The estimated reaction function (static, no intercept, off-floor month-ends) has the wrong sign on current data: USD a=-0.17, b=-0.51 on the full sample; the first estimate is a=4.97, b=3.44; GBP a is about -1.1 through 2022.

On current panels the level and slope signals move together (z correlation 0.85 USD, 0.94 GBP). Roll maintenance, not signal flips, drives turnover. The breakeven cost can be negative (USD) or above the 5bp grid (GBP). Unit-DV01 sleeve statistics are dominated by 2022-24. The credit bridge's Baa-minus-10y spread builds in a Treasury 10s30s maturity mismatch that could create a spurious policy-path link.

Most fixes are definitional, not rework: net positions per instrument, a no-view ELB state, a partial-adjustment estimated rule shrunk toward the imposed rule, exact formulas for IC, NW, turnover, breakeven and regimes, Sharpe standard errors everywhere, and maturity-matched credit spreads. The numbers were computed read-only from data/panel/*.parquet and data/cache/boe/GBP/OIS_SPOT.parquet. The turnover and breakeven figures are rough: they use the crude backtest's held-rate change as a proxy for the week-7 instruments.

-- MAJOR | 5.2 inverse-vol and mean-variance sizing with hysteresis

-- MAJOR | 6 metrics.ic
